Commuter students on campus have access to a variety of spaces and lounges to make their experience even better. Below you can find the different spaces available around campus.

Kimmel Commuter Lounge - 2nd floor of the Kimmel Center

Lipton Commuter Den - Bottom floor of Lipton Hall

Tandon Commuter Lounge - 10th floor of 2 MetroTech Center

Kitchen spaces can be found in a few areas around campus. These spaces are available to use to heat up or briefly store any food you may pack.

Kimmel 7th Floor Kitchen - Located in the lounge area, and includes a fridge and microwave

Lipton Commuter Den Kitchen - Located in the rear of the den, and includes a fridge and microwave

Bobst Library Snack Lounge - Located on the lower level 1, and includes microwaves

Tandon Commuter Lounge - Located in the rear of the lounge, and includes a fridge and microwave

Dibner Snack Lounge - Located in room 440 on the 4th floor of 5 MetroTech Center, and includes seating and a microwave
